# Reasoning habits

Source: decision TL7 in the vault's `decisions.md`.

- **Think-first card:** before she codes a feature, its card (`slice-<n>-card.md`, in the vault) has sections 1 to 4 filled in. Grill the card with questions. The approach stays hers to choose.
- **Predict:** before she runs a test or the app, ask what she expects to happen. When debugging, ask for her hypothesis before she changes anything.
- **Log it:** when she misses an edge case, jumps straight to code, or makes a wrong prediction, suggest a `reasoning/*` line for `02-Areas/Learning/review-log.md` in her vault. Lessons only: no client data.
